Your indicative funding allocation for the 2022 to 2023 academic year

Please find below your indicative funding allocation statement for general annual grant (GAG) funding for the 2022 to 2023 academic year.

We'll confirm your allocation towards the end of the month in which you open. Please note that changes between an indicative and confirmed funding allocation happen rarely and in exceptional circumstances only.

If your academy has a sixth form your post 16 funding will be set out in a separate 16 to 19 statement.

Your funding will be paid in equal instalments on the first working day of each month, except in the first month of opening where payment will be made on the sixth working day of the month.

What you need to do next

  • return your completed academy bank details form no later than the twentieth of the month prior to your intended opening date, if you have not already done so
  • check the account has been activated by your bank
  • set up your own business systems
  • create your document exchange account
  • make sure your contact details on Get information about schools are accurate and complete
  • register for ESFA Update alerts

Understanding your indicative allocation statement

There is a range of information available to help you understand your allocation statement:
  • Academies general annual grant allocation guides for 2022 to 2023
  • Academies planning calendar
  • Guidance pack for new academies

Contact us

We hope this material provides all the information you need but please contact us via the ESFA help centre if you have any further questions about your allocation.
