Notes
(1) The lower and upper thresholds are the percentages of pupils from your estimate beyond which the adjustment is calculated and are specified in your funding agreement. If not mentioned specifically in your funding agreement, these will be 0%.
(2) These are the pupil numbers used by the Education and Skills Funding Agency to calculate your funding for the academic year 2021 to 2022 and can be found in:
(i) Pre-16 - General Annual Grant (GAG) allocation statement - see Table A
(ii) Post-16 - 16-19 allocation statement - see 'Student numbers' table
(3) If your pupil numbers are below the lower threshold funded numbers, we will decrease your allocation by applying a pupil number adjustment based on the difference.
(4) If your pupil numbers are above the upper threshold funded numbers, we will increase your allocation by applying a pupil number adjustment based on the difference.
(5) This is the number of pre-16 (Years Reception to Year 11) and post-16 (Years 12 to 14) pupils from your October 2021 census returns. We use headcount for pre-16 and post-16 and only count those pupils registered as 'sole' or 'dual (main)'
(6) We have only calculated adjustments where your total census numbers for pre-16 and post-16 pupils (see footnote 5) are different from your total pre-16 and post-16 estimates by more than your threshold %. The number of pupils outside of the threshold for pre-16 and post-16 are calculated individually and adjustments are then calculated separately for each block.
(7) This is the funding allocation received in academic year 2021 to 2022, which is subject to adjustment as follows:
(i) Pre-16 - General Annual Grant (GAG) allocation statement - see Table A. This is the Minimum Funding Guarantee (MFG) budget after MFG and affordability adjustments. For academies opening from September 2021, this is the School Budget Share less the Lump Sum plus any Funding Protection.
(ii) Post-16 - 16-19 allocation statement - 'Core programme funding' in 'Summary of 2021/22 funding allocation'. This excludes post-16 high needs, student support (bursary) funding, and capacity and delivery funding.
(8) Allocation divided by the number of pupils for which your academy was funded
(9) This is the number of pupils outside of the lower or upper threshold, multiplied by the per pupil rate
(10) Where a local authority has already provided additional funding for basic need growth, the ESFA will deduct the amount from any positive pre-16 adjustment so the growth is only funded for once.
